Privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026

In short: Spendlight has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. We don't collect your data — it stays on your phone. If you choose to use the optional AI features, the information needed for each request goes directly from your phone to the AI provider you picked, and only after you allow it.

What we collect

Nothing. Spendlight (“we”) does not run a server, does not create accounts, and does not include analytics, advertising or tracking code. We never receive your subscriptions, statements, screenshots, API keys or anything else you enter.

What stays on your phone

  • Your subscriptions and settings — names, prices, billing dates, categories, notes, usage marks and budget — are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • AI API keys, if you add one, are stored in your device's secure keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android).
  • Screenshots and statements you import are read on your device to find subscriptions and are not uploaded or kept. Only the subscriptions you confirm are saved.
  • Reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and passcode checks are handled by your phone's operating system; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Optional AI features

AI features are off unless you add your own API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or Google (Gemini). The first time you use an AI feature, Spendlight asks your permission. Once you allow it, the app sends only what each request needs, directly from your phone to that provider over an encrypted connection:

  • statements and screenshots you choose to import with AI;
  • text you type into quick add;
  • your subscription list (names, prices, billing cycles, categories and usage) when you ask for an AI review.

We are not involved in these requests and cannot see them. The provider handles them under its own terms and privacy policy, and may keep them for a limited time: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google. You can withdraw permission or remove your key at any time in Settings → AI provider.

Other connections

Links you tap — such as a service's cancellation page — open in your browser, subject to that website's own policy. When you export a backup or CSV, the file goes wherever you choose in your phone's share sheet.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Remove a saved AI key in Settings → AI provider. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Spendlight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
