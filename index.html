Help and support

Spendlight shows what your subscriptions really cost, what renews next, and which ones you could cancel. Everything stays on your phone.

Need help or found a problem? Email sizoperationz@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what you were doing, and include a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

How do I add my subscriptions without typing them all?

Take a screenshot of your subscriptions list — on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Then in Spendlight, tap Scan screenshots and choose it. Spendlight reads it on your phone and shows what it found for you to confirm. You can also import a bank statement (CSV) or paste its text.

Do I need an AI account?

No. Scanning screenshots and importing statements work entirely on your phone. AI features are optional: if you add your own API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or Google (Gemini), Spendlight can also read PDFs and messy layouts and review your subscriptions. The provider bills your account directly for what you use.

Can Spendlight cancel a subscription for me?

No app can do that — each service handles its own billing. Spendlight takes you straight to the right cancellation page, and to the App Store or Google Play subscriptions page for plans billed there.

Why doesn't a total match my bank statement?

The monthly figure is an average: yearly plans are spread across twelve months and weekly plans are scaled up. Tap Monthly spend on the Overview to see exactly what is charged in each month. Other currencies are converted at the rates in Settings, which you can edit.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Settings → Data to export a backup or a CSV. Spendlight has no accounts or cloud sync, so your data lives only on your phone.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. To remove a saved AI key, open Settings → AI provider and remove it.
